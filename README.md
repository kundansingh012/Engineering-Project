# Kundan Singh Shekhawat — Mechatronics & Automation Projects

I am a Master's student in Mechatronics and Automation Engineering at Macquarie University, with over **6 years of industry experience** in mechanical design and product development for **electronic medical devices and special-purpose machinery**. I build systems that combine mechanical design, electronics and software, and I am a co-author of a research paper accepted at **ICST 2026**.

This repository holds selected projects in robotics, industrial automation, embedded systems and machine learning.
